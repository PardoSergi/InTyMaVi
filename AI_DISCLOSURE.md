# AI Disclosure

## Overview

InTyMaVi was developed using AI-assisted programming ("vibe coding").
The program was developed substantially through interactions with large language
models, primarily Claude and, to a lesser extent, ChatGPT and DeepSeek.

## How AI was used

AI assistance was used for tasks including:

- generating and modifying code;
- implementing new features;
- debugging and troubleshooting;
- drafting and proofreading documentation and communication.

The final code was reviewed and tested by the author, but the project was
developed through an iterative process of prompting, inspecting, testing,
and modifying AI-generated code.

## Human responsibility

The author is responsible for the final implementation, including decisions
about functionality, research use, and the contents of the released versions.

AI-generated suggestions and code were not assumed to be correct without
testing or inspection.

## Prompts

Because the project was developed over an extended period and prompts were
distributed across multiple conversations and AI systems, a complete record
of all prompts was not maintained from the beginning.

I am therefore documenting the prompts that can be recovered from the
development history. Where relevant, prompts are provided in their original
Catalan version together with an English translation. You will find them in
`AI_PROMPTS.md`.

## Reproducibility and transparency

This document is intended to make the role of AI in the development of
InTyMaVi transparent. It may be updated as additional information about the
development process is recovered or documented.
